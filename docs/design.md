# 2026-09 homepage design

Audience: prospective students and research collaborators seeking a geophysics lab's work, people, publications and contact details.

Palette: paper #FFFFFF; depth #16354B; water #135D86; mist #EEF4F7; text #203747; secondary #526879. The ocean/depth colors relate to the lab's seismic and underwater-acoustics work. Avoid decorative gradients and repeated card shadows.

Typography: Noto Sans KR for Korean/body, Georgia for the short English lab title. Body 16–18px, headings 30–58px, bibliographic text 15–17px. Left alignment and readable measures throughout.

Layout: a large, sourced scientific figure next to the research introduction; a featured latest paper below; three research themes with links into existing detailed sections; recent news and recruitment at the end. Internal pages share a quiet title area and navigation. Publication rows remain complete scholarly citations with year filters.

Plan review: replaced the original logo-as-hero, fake stats, repeated icon cards and decorative reveal animations with an actual paper figure and concrete research content. Keep the existing research explanations, with larger type and less visual chrome. Do not present manuscript results as published work.

Desktop: [introduction | scientific figure] / [latest paper] / [research links] / [news | contact]
Mobile: introduction / figure / latest paper / research links / news / contact.

Verification: inspect desktop and phone screenshots, keyboard focus, filters, navigation resizing and JavaScript-disabled content. The research-project tab remains deferred.

## Landing-page correction after user review

The user rejected the scientific-figure hero and requested a conventional opening. The home page now opens with the lab name, affiliation, a brief research description and ordinary navigation links. Scientific figures stay on the Research page; the latest paper follows the overview of research themes. This feedback supersedes the initial hero proposal above.
